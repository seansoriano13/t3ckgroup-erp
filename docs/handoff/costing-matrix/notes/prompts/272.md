Over ABC by one centavo fails.
- Add a "detail" to condition results and show the failing checks, in plain words, in the Stage checklist row (StageChecklist / StageChecklistRow) — across every place opportunityService builds condition results.
- Live: the condition re-evaluates whenever the checklist is computed; an edit clearing approval or the Bid Decision makes it unmet.
- Bid submitted: when a move leaves a Stage through a met costing_settled, set costing_matrices.bid_submitted_at if empty (where the move is recorded — find the existing path). After that, a Final Offer change writes a sales_opportunity_logs entry on the open visit (trigger on costing_tender_line_prices; before bid_submitted_at, nothing).
- Tests: unit (each failing check alone and together; one centavo over fails; no Tender Lines fails; no matrix fails); integration (fake PostgREST: blocks a move until the last check passes; an Offer Specification edit after approval blocks it again; Final Offer change after bid_submitted_at lands on the timeline, before doesn't); Flow Canvas picker offers it and a published version shows it; page test: the Stage checklist lists failing checks; policy for costing_condition_inputs (any caller who can see the SOR gets only booleans/counts).
