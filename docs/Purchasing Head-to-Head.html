Part 6 · Round 1

Open decisions, each with a recommendation

These ten can be answered now; none depends on another. The exit test for PMS Sourcing GS waits on Q1 and comes in round 2.

Q1

What PMS Sourcing GS becomes once the Costing Matrix ships

Conflict

Keep the stage, retire its sheet link. It is one deal waiting on pricing, which ADR 0023 allows. The required "GS 1 CRM GDrive" field goes when the matrix lands, so nobody keeps a second sheet.

Q2

How long Technical keeps the SOR after SO Evaluation

Conflict

Set the Evaluation task's window to "until For Bidding is left". The engine already has that option, so this is a Flow Canvas edit, not code. Technical then sees the matrix while Components exist and until Costing settled is checked.

Q3

When the Purchase Request opens on Government

Conflict

Twice, idempotently: an Optional Action at Lead (Deal Owner decides) for inquiries, and automatic at NTP for buying. Amend #227 from "after award" to "by award at the latest". The 18 hand-made inquiry tasks show the need starts at Lead.

Q4

What Raquel's "assign a purchasing assistant" is

Gap

The matrix's Purchasing Incharge. A Canvass on a Component defaults its Canvasser to that row's Incharge, then to the Buyer. For Bidding's "Purchasing Involved" text field retires.

Q5

Whether purchasing views show the client's delivery deadline

Gap

Yes, read-only. Show NTP's Delivery Deadline beside the SOR's orders in Order Monitoring and the Dashboard's On order group, and mark an order not yet received as the deadline nears. Blocks nothing. One small ticket under #261.

Q6

What "PO" means

Gap

"Purchase Order" is T3CK's Odoo order to a supplier; the client's is a "Client PO". Purchasing screens say "Odoo PO". The CA / PO stage keeps its name; Operations owns it.

Q7

Where Purchasing settled sits on Government

Gap

On leaving Project Delivery. Deliveries go out partly; the stage just can't close while an item is still open.

Q8

Who gets purchasing access, and when

Gap

Grant on the day the #209 block reaches prod, not before, so nobody lands on the old queue. Purchasing: Raquel, Cjay, Ayla. Final Acquire list: 101. Evaluation list: Raquel. Finance: Jelai. Check by: 1001 and 102.

Q9

Which Closed Book tier Always sees gets

Gap

The price side, same as the Deal Team. Always-sees salespeople already read quotation prices on the SOR; ABC, Markup Price and Final Offer leak nothing new.

Q10

What happens to the 18 hand-made "PMS" tasks

Gap

No migration. At switch-over Raquel closes each with a note, and re-raises any still-live inquiry as a Requested Item on that SOR's Purchase Request. A status change, never a delete.
