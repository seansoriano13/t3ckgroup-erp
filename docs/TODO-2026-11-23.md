## 1. Payments (IPAS and the GM's Payment Forecast)

### 1.1 Payment status on every IPAS
- **Problem:** The GM (201) releases supplier payments but can't tell from an IPAS whether it needs paying. For example, TGIPAS_064 reads Order-Draft, yet it is fully paid.
- **Decided:** A second status beside the IPAS Status, with four steps:
  - **Not due yet**
  - **For payment:** set by Purchasing with "Ask for payment", giving the amount and what it is for. Recording the payment clears it.
  - **DP paid:** worked out from the payments recorded.
  - **Paid:** worked out from the payments recorded.
- On-Hold stays the IPAS's own.
- **Users will see:** a second pill on the IPAS record, an "Ask for payment" button for Purchasing, and a Payment status column and filter on the IPAS list.

### 1.2 Payment Forecast for the GM
- **Decided:** The Payment Forecast is the IPAS list filtered to For payment. It is not a new table.
  - The GM only reads it.
  - The GM sees a count on the sidebar's IPAS item, which already shows for her because she is an IPAS signer.
  - Everyone who can open an IPAS can see it.
- **Not included:**
  - purchases with no IPAS
  - Odoo's own bill status
  - withholding tax (BIR 2307)
- The Google Sheet (`docs/gsheet/payment-forecast.md`) was background only.

### 1.3 Suggest Payment Approval from the P.I

- It never moves by itself (ADR 0054).
- Today, a Prepared section 1 already suggests it. This adds a second trigger.

  - What the supplier is owed becomes the P.I/QT Amount if typed. If it isn't typed, it becomes the P0 total plus supplier-billed Freight.
  - The supplier bill check compares the P0 plus that Freight with the P.I.
  - Supplier-billed Freight is added to the Landed Cost once. Today it is skipped because it was assumed to be on the P0.
- **Live data:** Only one other shipping fee payment exists, on TGIPAS_062, which is cancelled. There is nothing to move.

## 4. Bugs (cause found)

### 4.1 Board shows the wrong colors
- **Cause:** The Board knows only 7 of the 20 stage colors. Every other color, like teal, jade or indigo, falls back to grey. Flow Canvas shows the right colors.
  - `src/components/sor/OpportunityBoard.jsx:19-27` and `:520`
  - The employee SOR matrix has the same problem: `src/components/sor/EmployeeOpportunityMatrix.jsx:38-46`
- **Fix:** Use the full color list in `src/constants/stageColors.js`.

### 4.2 Sidebar numbers don't match their pages
- **Example:** The sidebar showed Task Approvals 28, while the Approval Center's All Pending showed 1.
- **Cause:** The sidebar counts on its own, in `src/hooks/useNavBadges.js:33-52`.
  - For a Super Admin it counts every pending task in the company, but the page opens on "My team".
  - It also adds every completed task that HR hasn't verified.
- **Fix:** Each sidebar number uses the same count as its page's default view. Check the leave, Activities, IPAS and My SORs numbers the same way.

### 4.3 Notification bell number
- **Cause:** The bell counts only the newest 50 notifications.
  - The phone badge has no "+".
  - "Mark all read" updates only the ones loaded on screen.
  - Notifications in tabs you can't see still count.
  - Code: `src/services/notificationService.js:53-68`, `src/components/SideNav.jsx:436-440` and `:663-670`.
- **Fix:** Count unread notifications on the server, using the same rules as the list.

### 4.4 "Removing a product adds to the IPAS amount" (TGIPAS_064)
- **Not a calculation bug.** The amount from Odoo went *down* by €1,600 when the Shipping Cost line was removed.
- The real problem: shipping was counted twice, once inside the 60% and 40% payments and again as a €1,600 shipping fee payment. The app had no right place for shipping the supplier bills.
- **Fixed by 1.4.**
