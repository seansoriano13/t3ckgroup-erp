A. BUGS IN THE APP
======================================================================
[A1] A new SOR's S0 Evaluation opens empty.
  Where: Register SOR-256 on S03204 > PMS tab > Open S0 Evaluation.
  What: "Lines 0", "No S0 lines yet", though the SOR has 12 products from Odoo.
        It opens at registration, before the S0 save step (#815/#816) pulls the
        products. "Refresh from the SOR" adds them (costing_fill_from_sor returned
        added: 12) but the screen shows 0 until a reload. The Odoo costs only fill on
        that first fill or a later Refresh, so after a reload the costs stay blank
        until someone presses Refresh again.

[A2] The Canvass window opens behind the line panel.
  Where: S0 Evaluation > open line 1 > Canvass.
  What: The record window is at layer 70 (src/components/ui/ModalShell.jsx:109), the
        line panel at layer 75 (src/components/ui/SlideOver.jsx:62, overModal). The
        Canvass cannot be clicked. Workaround: Purchasing > Canvasses.

[A3] Purchasing sees a broken header on the S0 Evaluation.
  Where: same sheet, as Cjay.
  What: Title "SOR · S0 Evaluation" with no SOR number, client "-", Sales Incharge
        "Former team member" instead of "Dani demo". Dani sees all three correctly.

[A4] A Canvass offer saves with no price.
  Where: Canvass > Add offer > type a supplier > press Enter.
  What: Enter submits at once: supplier name only, no currency, no price.

[A5] The server lets a Received line take another P0.
  Where: odoo-po-verify, action "attach", on the fire suit (Received on P01046).
  What: Attaching P01421 (the goggles P0) was accepted: the line became "P01421,
        bought as goggles", linked to TGIPAS_068. Re-attaching P01046 fixed the P0
        but left the line on TGIPAS_068 (fixed by hand in the local DB). The screen
        hides Attach P0 on a Received line, but the server does not refuse it.

[A6] Stage gates are only checked in the browser.
  Where: SOR-256 on Project Delivery, as Dani.
  What: A direct update of flow_stage_id to Collection was accepted, with "Purchasing
        settled" unmet and both required uploads missing. sor_guard_stage_move only
        checks access and restricted stages; every condition (purchasing gates,
        uploads, fields) is enforced only by opportunityService.assertCanLeaveStage.
        Anyone with the app's client can skip a gate. (Moved back locally.)

[A7] The section 4 refusal names an internal number.
  Where: ipas_sign, Grace "Received" before Logistics.
  What: "section 4 opens once sections 1, 2, 3 and 6 are signed". "6" is the stored
        number; users know sections 1-4 and Logistics.

[A8] Section 2 says "Complete" while charges wait.
  Where: IPAS TGIPAS_067 > Edit > section 2.
  What: The summary says "All 2 required fields filled. Complete" while both blank
        charges say "Fill the amount or remove this charge. Sections 2 and 3 wait".

[A9] The SOR header stays stale after saving stage fields (still there from 2026-10-29).
  What: After saving Bidder, Representative and Mode of Pre Bid, the header kept
        "Blocked: 3 - missing ..." and clicking the next stage did nothing until a
        reload. Same on SO Evaluation ("Blocked: 2" vs "Nothing blocks this stage").
        At Project Delivery it said "Ready to move to Collection" with gates unmet.

[A10] (small) A request line jumps to the bottom after its status changes.

[A11] A forward move to a Stage the deal visited before counts as a "move back", and a
  move back skips the gates.
  Where: SOR-256 on Project Delivery, after A6's test had put it on Collection once.
  What: Clicking Collection opened "Move back to Collection? Resume / Start fresh",
        and getReturnStageIds makes assertCanLeaveStage skip the gates. So a deal that
        once reached a later Stage (by a revert, or A6) can go forward again past
        Purchasing settled and the required uploads. Cancelled; not confirmed.
