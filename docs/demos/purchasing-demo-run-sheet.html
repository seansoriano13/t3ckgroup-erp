Purchasing Demo Run Sheet

One real government deal, from Lead to a filed IPAS, told from Purchasing's side. The deal is Odoo sale order S03204: Marikina City Government, fire-fighting equipment, 12 product lines, ₱1,452,768. It is bought on four real P0s: two in dollars from China, two in pesos from local suppliers. Part 0 is done alone before the meeting. Parts 1 to 9 are the one-hour demo. Parts A to E are side scenarios you can slip in or run on their own. Tick each step as you go. Ticks stay in this browser only, and "Clear ticks" starts a fresh rehearsal. The counter at the top counts the main demo only.

Rewritten 2026-10-29 (not yet rehearsed). What changed since the last demo: the Costing Matrix is now the S0 Evaluation, Final Acquire, Prepared, Costed and Final approved are gone, a Canvass needs three supplier slots and is no longer signed, attaching a P0 raises its IPAS by itself, buying opens at NTP, the IPAS has a Logistics sign-off, and the Dashboard is one Tracker.

Know before you start

The four P0s

P01046 Jiujang Fire Fighting, USD 3,223: suit, gloves, belt, boots, hood, both helmets. All arrived.
P01421 TG Enterprise, ₱13,860: goggles. All arrived.
P01047 Taizhou Sanjiang, USD 1,380: 1.5" hose (10 of 10 arrived), 2.5" nozzle (0 of 4), 2.5" NH hose ×8 not on the S0 (0 of 8), and a Guangzhou shipping line.
P01423 TG Global Trade, ₱21,840: 2.5" hose (2 of 10 for the S0 line), 1.5" nozzle ×2 (arrived).

Why the deal will not close

Odoo says the client got everything, but the P0s only show part of the 2.5" hose and none of the 2.5" nozzles received. The app trusts the P0s, so the "Purchasing settled" gate refuses Collection at the end. That is a real finding, not a bug. Say it out loud in Part 8.

Cancelled P0s

S03204 also has five cancelled P0s in Odoo (P01415, P01416, P01418, P01419, P01425). They are the material for the refusal in Scenario E.

The dollar rate

Peso figures use the Reference Rate for the day the app reads it. Every peso figure in this sheet is "about", so read the screen, not the sheet.

Setup, before the meeting

about 10 min, alone

The reset script now fills every sign-off list and the "Government" markup by itself, so Part 0 is only the flow. Run step 0.1 again before each rehearsal.

  1. TerminalPull staging, then: node scripts/demo-purchasing-setup.mjs --apply
    "Committed." The cast is named "… demo". The IPAS, Transmittal and S0 Evaluation lists are filled (see the Logins table). "Government" 1.30 is the only active markup. The old copy of the flow is renamed "(old …)" and its SORs hidden.
    It runs on your PC and writes straight to the staging database (STAGING_DB_URL in .env.local). Nothing is deployed.
  2. 101Open the Flow Canvas (/super-admin/flow-canvas) → Project [FOR DEMO]. Add only these:
    • Lead → Action "Open Purchase Request", When confirmed, Decider = the Deal Owner and the Sales Director. No Buyer. Access window: until Collection is left.
    • Lead → Action "Open S0 Evaluation", automatic: Sales incharge = Deal Owner, Purchasing incharge = Cjay demo, markup preset = Government.
    • PMS Sourcing GS → Condition "Costing priced".
    • For Bidding → Condition "Costing settled".
    • NTP → toggle "Buying opens here".
    • Project Delivery → Action "Request Packing List", Conditions "Purchasing settled" and "Goods checked".
  3. 101Publish the flow with the note "Purchasing demo".
    Project [FOR DEMO] can now be picked when registering an SOR.
  4. 101Settings → Purchasing: check the IPAS charge kinds. Each active kind becomes a blank charge on a new dollar IPAS, so keep only the ones you will fill (Freight, Brokerage).
  5. Open the four seat windows and sign in: dani@demo, cjay@demo, grace@demo (Signers), aldrin@demo. Keep the PDF folder open.

The big picture

0:00 – 0:04
  1. PurchasingOpen the Purchasing menu: Dashboard, Purchase Requests, Canvasses, IPAS, Transmittals, Packing Lists.
    Purchasing has no stages of its own. The deal moves through Sales stages, and those stages open our work: a Purchase Request and an S0 Evaluation. Everything after that is records: Canvass, P0, IPAS, Transmittal, Packing List.
  2. Three rules to say out loud.
    One: Odoo is the truth for the S0, the P0 and what arrived. The app reads Odoo and never writes to it. Two: every SOR has one product list, and Sales and Purchasing see the same status on each line. Three: it is a one-way road. Once something is signed or decided, changing it asks for a reason.
