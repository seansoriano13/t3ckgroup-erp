Purchasing Demo Run Sheet

One government Project deal, from Lead to filed IPAS, told from Purchasing's side. The deal is a real Odoo sale order, S03885 (Tagbilaran City Government, Portable Light Tower Kit), with its real purchase orders P01235 (the light towers) and P01317 (bubble wrap). Part 0 is done alone before the meeting. Parts 1 to 9 are the one-hour demo. Parts A to C are side scenarios you can slip in or run on their own. Tick each step as you go. Ticks stay in this browser only, and "Clear ticks" starts a fresh rehearsal. The counter at the top counts the main demo only.

Updated 2026-10-26 (not yet rehearsed). The Purchase Request lists the S0 lines by itself, statuses use the new words, the Canvass starts from the product line, and Purchased and Received follow Odoo. Checked against Odoo today: S03885 has eight product lines, not five, because each kit lists its battery, charger and cable on its own lines. P01235 is fully received in Odoo (March 2025), so its lines turn Received as soon as it is attached.

Know before you start

Received straight away

P01235 arrived in full in March 2025. When you attach it in step 6.1, the six lines go straight to Received. To show "Purchased, not here yet", use Scenario C, whose P0s are still on the way.

Skip the Actual cost on this deal

P01235 is in US dollars. The matrix shows the P0's dollar price as if it were pesos, so the light tower 400 would read "Expected ₱24,641.49 · Actual ₱388.00 (−98.4%)". This is a known bug. Do not open the matrix's cost detail after step 6.1.

The dollar rate

All peso figures use 63.509 pesos per dollar (ECB, 2026-10-22). The app reads the rate for the day, so the numbers move a little if the rate changed.

Landed Cost

The IPAS and the matrix show today's Landed Cost. The full Landed Cost computation is on the roadmap, so present the figure as a first version.

The big picture

0:00 – 0:05
  1. PurchasingOpen the Purchasing menu and show the pages: Purchase Requests, Canvasses, Dashboard, IPAS, Transmittals, Packing Lists.
    Purchasing has no stages of its own. The deal (SOR) moves through Sales stages, and those stages create our work: a Purchase Request and a Costing Matrix. Everything after that is records: Canvass, IPAS, Transmittal, Packing List.
  2. Three rules to say out loud.
    One: Odoo is the truth for the S0, the P0, what arrived and stock. The app reads Odoo and never writes to it, and nobody types those facts twice. Two: every SOR has one product list, and Sales and Purchasing see the same status on each product. Three: what you can see depends on your access, not your department name.

C

One IPAS for two projects

about 10 min, on its own

Real case from Odoo. Two fire vehicle deals share their roof parts. P02674 (USD 150, magnetic roof mounts) and P02587 (USD 202.80, ethernet adapters, pivot mounts, wall mounts) are both from Shenzhen Xingshuo Guanglian Technology, both name S04081 and S04602 in their Source Document, and neither has arrived yet. This shows several P0s, several IPAS, one IPAS for two projects, and "Purchased, not here yet".

  1. SalesRegister two SORs on Project [FOR DEMO] with Fetch from Odoo: S04081 (BFP Command Bus Vehicle ×4, National Headquarters BFP) and S04602 (Fire Chief Command Vehicle ×1, Barangay Wack-Wack). Create the Purchase Request on each.
    Each request has one S0 line: the vehicle. Mark it To purchase.
  2. PurchasingOn the S04081 request, Ask for a product: "Magnetic roof mount", quantity 4, Material for the job. Attach P0 → type P02674 (S04081 has almost 200 P0s, so typing is quicker than finding it in the list).
    Purchased with "Not received yet · ETA 2026-10-08". The ready bar does not count it as ready.
    Purchased means bought, not here yet. The ETA is Odoo's Expected Arrival, which today is often just the order date. When Odoo receives it, the line turns Received by itself the next time the request opens.
  3. PurchasingDo the same on the S04602 request: "Magnetic roof mount", quantity 1, attach P02674 the same way.
    The same P0 sits on two projects' product lists.
  4. PurchasingNew IPAS: PO P02674, Source overseas, Purpose Project.
    The SORs box already holds both S04081 and S04602, read from the Source Document. Keep both and create.
    One IPAS can serve several projects. Each SOR's PMS tab shows it. If you keep only one SOR, the IPAS warns that Odoo's S numbers differ from its SORs.
  5. PurchasingSecond P0 for the same two projects: ask for "Ethernet adapter and mounts" on S04081, attach P02587, then New IPAS for P02587.
    Two IPAS, each naming both SORs. S04081's PMS tab lists both.
  6. SalesMove S04081 to Project Delivery, then try Collection.
    Refused by "Purchasing settled": the roof mounts are Purchased, not Received.
    This is the gate catching a deal that would close before its parts arrived.
Watch out. P02674 lists the roof mount on two lines (4 and 1). The app reads only the first line for a product, so the S04602 line will say "0 of 4 received", not "0 of 1". This is a known gap. Also try attaching P02181: it is cancelled in Odoo, so it is refused.

More edge cases, not yet scripted

RFQ, not confirmed

Attach a P0 that is still a draft in Odoo, like P02679 (₱165, names S04081). The line stays To purchase with an "RFQ P0…" note, so an unconfirmed order never looks bought.

Direct request for a project

Open a Direct request, "Spare chargers for the demo kits", and name S03885 as the project. It stays Direct, gets its own PR number, and shows on the SOR under "Direct requests for this project".

Is this yours?

Needs Sales to add a product to the S0 in Odoo after Purchasing bought it as an Other request "For the client". The new S0 line asks "Is this from your purchase request?". Skip it unless you have a test quotation, because the app never writes to Odoo and we should not edit a real one.

Removed from S0

Also needs an S0 edit in Odoo. When a line leaves the S0, it stays on the list marked "Removed from S0", with its P0 and history.
