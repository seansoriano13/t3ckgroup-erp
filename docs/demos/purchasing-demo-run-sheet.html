Purchasing Demo Run Sheet

One real government deal, from Lead to a filed IPAS, told from Purchasing's side. The deal is Odoo sale order S03204: Marikina City Government, fire-fighting equipment, 12 product lines, ₱1,370,535.74. It is bought on four real P0s. Part 0 is setup, done alone. Parts 1 to 9 are the one-hour demo. Parts A to F are side scenarios. Tick each step as you go. Ticks and the numbers you write down stay in this browser only.

Rewritten 2026-10-29, second pass. Every button name, field and gate below was checked against the code on staging and against the staging database that day. It has not been rehearsed in the app yet. The fixes from the first version: the setup now says every field to fill, each stage move lists what it asks for, the gates are explained by where they really bite, and the Transmittal and Canvass steps follow the real screens.

How to read a step

Five rows, always in this order

Who is the seat and login. Where is the screen, written as menu → page → part of the page. Do is what to click or type. See is what the screen should show. Say is the line for the room.

Gates check the stage you leave

A gate on a stage stops the deal from leaving that stage. It never stops the deal from arriving. So "Costing priced" on PMS Sourcing GS is checked when Sales moves PMS Sourcing GS → Proposal Submitted.

Four seats, four windows

One browser window keeps one login. Sales in Chrome. Purchasing in Chrome Incognito. Signers in Edge, where you sign out and in between Jelai, Jen, Grace, 201 and 101. Warehouse in Edge InPrivate (Aldrin and Sherwin).

One demo file

Every upload uses docs/demos/purchasing-demo-files/demo-document.pdf. All four P0s already exist in Odoo and name S03204. Do not make new ones.

How the demo ends

Odoo says the client got everything, but the P0s show only part of the 2.5" hose and none of the 2.5" nozzles received. The app trusts the P0s, so "Purchasing settled" refuses Project Delivery → Collection at the very end. That is a real finding in our records, not a bug. Say it out loud in step 8.5.

Cancelled P0s

S03204 also has five cancelled P0s in Odoo: P01415, P01416, P01418, P01419, P01425. Scenario E uses P01415 to show a refusal.

Peso figures are "about"

Dollar costs turn into pesos at the Reference Rate of the day. Read the screen, not this sheet, for any peso number.

Group Tasks open by themselves

Several stages open a Group Task for the real teams (BIDCOM, PB Team, DELCOM and so on). Ignore them. Only PQ asks first: press Skip there.
