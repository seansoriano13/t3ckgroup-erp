Purchasing Demo Run Sheet

One government Project deal, from Lead to filed IPAS, told from Purchasing's side. The deal is a real Odoo sale order, S03885 (Tagbilaran City Government, Portable Light Tower Kit), and its real purchase order, P01235. Part 0 is done alone before the meeting. Parts 1 to 9 are the one-hour demo. Tick each step as you go. Ticks stay in this browser only, and "Clear ticks" starts a fresh rehearsal.

Updated 2026-10-23: the demo now uses the real sale order S03885 and its five products, instead of the generator and radio story. Purchase order P01235 already exists in Odoo, so nobody makes it during the demo. All peso figures use today's rate, 63.509 pesos per dollar (ECB, 2026-10-22). Check the rate again on the day, because the app reads the rate for that date.

The big picture

0:00 – 0:05
  1. PurchasingOpen the Purchasing menu and show the pages: Purchase Requests, Canvasses, Dashboard, IPAS, Transmittals, Packing Lists.
    Purchasing has no stages of its own. The deal (SOR) moves through Sales stages, and those stages create our work: a Purchase Request and a Costing Matrix. Everything after that is records: Canvass, IPAS, Transmittal, Packing List.
  2. Three rules to say out loud.
    One: we never write to Odoo, we only read it. POs are still made in Odoo, and this deal's PO was made there before the demo. Two: what you can see depends on your access, not your department name. Three: nothing is "the purchasing stage". The Dashboard works out what is waiting from the records.

3

The Purchase Request

0:08 – 0:14
  1. PurchasingPurchase Requests list. Show the new request with no Buyer. Click Take.
    Nobody approves a request. Whoever has Purchasing access takes it. Every purchaser stays a member, so nobody loses sight of it.
  2. SalesIn the request chat, ask: "@Cjay do we have Portable Light Towers available, and how much?"
    This is how an inquiry works at Lead. The chat cannot be edited or deleted, by anyone. It is the record.
  3. PurchasingReply in the chat: "Battery packs are in stock. The light towers, charger and cable need a canvass, I'll price them."
  4. SalesAdd Requested Items → pick all five SOR lines.
    Items are copied from the SOR. If Sales edits the SOR later, our item does not change by itself.
  5. PurchasingSet the battery pack item to From stock (88 on hand in Odoo). Point at the Undo button in the message, then let it go. Set the light tower 400, light tower 200, charger and cable items to To order.
    From stock closes the item. Undo works for about 10 seconds, only for the person who did it. The four To order items are the ones on the Odoo purchase order.
Dashboard check. Purchasing Dashboard → Projects tab. The SOR is in To source: four items at To order.
4

Costing Matrix and Canvass

0:14 – 0:22
  1. SalesMove the SOR Lead → SO Evaluation.
    The PMS tab now shows a Costing Matrix card with an Open Costing Matrix button.
  2. SalesClick Open Costing Matrix. Open each line and type its ABC / unit:
    • Line 1, light tower 400: ₱160,625 (₱642,500 for 4)
    • Line 2, light tower 200: ₱94,553.57 (₱567,321.42 for 6)
    • Lines 3 to 5, battery, charger, cable: ₱0, because the sale order includes them in the tower price
    The matrix opens in its own window and fills itself: "5 lines added from the SOR", one line per product, each "priced from its product".
    Nobody retypes the products. Sales owns the tender side: what the client asked for and the budget (ABC). The battery, charger and cable have no separate budget, so their ABC stays at zero.
  3. PurchasingOpen the same matrix (PMS tab, or the Costing Matrix page). Open each line and type the product's Brand: Coming. In the request, link the light tower 400 item to line 1, and the light tower 200 item to line 2.
    One product per line is the normal case. We fill in the Brand, because we inquire with suppliers. The link is how the PO and Landed Cost find their way back to the matrix. Each column belongs to a team, and the system refuses edits outside your columns.
  4. PurchasingOpen lines 3, 4 and 5 and type the Acquire, taken from our Odoo purchase order at 63.509 pesos a dollar: battery ₱3,683.52, charger ₱2,476.85, cable ₱3,111.94.
    The battery comes from stock, so there is no canvass. It still needs a cost, because every line must be costed before "Costing priced" waits for every product to pass.
  5. PurchasingOpen line 1 and start a Canvass on the light tower 400. Add one Offer: Shenzhen Coming Technology Co., Ltd., USD 388 a unit, the price on our purchase order. The comparison rate stays at the pre-filled 63.509. Do the same for line 2 (light tower 200) with USD 179.
    The number CNV_… appears only when the first Offer is saved. The USD Offer shows a peso figure from the comparison rate, and the app warns that there are fewer than three Offers.
    We only have one real quote per tower, so the warning is expected. Say it out loud: fewer than three only warns. The comparison rate only compares Offers. It is not the rate we pay.
  6. PurchasingOn both canvasses, mark Shenzhen Coming Technology as the winner, reason "Only quote we have, same supplier as our PO P01235".
    Line 1's Acquire fills in from the winner (about ₱24,641.49). Line 2's Acquire fills in too (about ₱11,368.11).
  7. jenApproval Center → Canvass sign-offs → sign both Canvasses.
    The canvasser can never sign their own Canvass. Editing a signed Canvass removes the signature.

  2. PurchasingIPAS list → New IPAS: PO P01235, Priority High, Source the overseas option, Purpose Project, SOR = S03885.
    TGIPAS_… with the supplier, lines and totals pulled from Odoo by the server.

  3. PurchasingBack on the light tower 400 item, pick the PO line (5 units at USD 388).
    PO lines come from the IPAS copy, so the IPAS comes first. This link is what brings Landed Cost back to the matrix. The PO has one more tower than the SOR asks for. Say so if someone asks.
  4. PurchasingEdit the IPAS: add a charge "Delivery" ₱5,000, type the supplier bank details, tick "Purchasing has it" on two documents. Save all.
    One Edit, one long form, one Save. Only Purchasing types bank details. A changed account number is flagged and clears section 2.
  5. jelaiOpen the IPAS, Edit → add a payment: Full payment, USD 10,057 (the PO total), method from the list, proof = demo PDF. Tick "Finance has it" on the same documents. Save all.
    "Paid before approval" mark, because section 1 is not approved yet. The app shows the peso figure from the rate on the payment date, about ₱638,709 at 63.509. The With P.O.P tick turns on.
  6. PurchasingSubmit section 1 and section 2.
  7. 101Approval Center → IPAS → approve sections 1 and 2.
    The Sales Director approves sections 1 and 2. Nobody approves their own work, and even a Super Admin can only sign when they are on the list.
Dashboard check. Projects tab: the SOR is in On order. "What needs doing" tab: the IPAS shows the next step and who owes it.
