Item Road demo: SOR-250, Fire fighting equipment

A real deal on staging: S0 S03204, 12 lines, purchase request PR_0171, and the real Odoo P0 P01046, which covers 7 of the 12 items. Tick each box as you go.

What you are checking. Each line of the S0 Evaluation now says where it is on the road: Planned → Go → Requested → Ordered → Paying → Cost final → Received. Before this change, SOR-250's twelve purchase items never reached the S0 Evaluation at all, because they were made from the S0's lines and not "from the S0 Evaluation".
Before you start. This runs on staging, not the live product. Step 7 writes to staging: it marks one purchase item Purchased on P01046 and creates a new IPAS for it there. Odoo is only read, never changed. The old IPAS for P01046 (TGIPAS_063) is cancelled, so a new one is expected.

Part 1: put the change on staging

  1. Ask Claude to merge the feat/item-road branch into staging and push it. GitHub runs "Apply migrations — staging" and it finishes green; the staging site rebuilds a few minutes later.

Part 2: see the road

  1. Open https://t3ckgroup-erp-staging.vercel.app and sign in as super-admin@mock.t3ck.test, password MockStaging#2026.
  2. Open SOR-250, then its S0 Evaluation. every one of the 12 lines has a blue Requested badge under its name. (Before this change they had no badge, and the parts showed nothing being bought.)
  3. Click the first item, FFS Fire Suit Aramid. In the side panel, open its part. a row of steps at the top of the part, with Requested lit and Planned and Go before it.
  4. Close the panel.

Part 3: order one item and watch it move

  1. Sign out, then sign in as the purchaser: purchaser@mock.t3ck.test, same password. (The super admin may not see the Attach P0 button; the purchaser does.)
  2. Go to Purchasing → Purchase Requests → PR_0171. Open the line FFS Fire Suit Aramid and press Attach P0. Pick P01046, and pick its Fire Suit line when asked. the item becomes Purchased on P01046, and a message says an IPAS was created.
  3. Go back to SOR-250 → S0 Evaluation. line 1 now says Paying (purple). The other 11 lines still say Requested.
  4. Open line 1 and its part again. Paying lit, and under the steps: "Paid through TGIPAS_… (P01046, Open)", which links to the new IPAS.
  5. Click that IPAS link. the IPAS for P01046 opens, naming SOR-250. This is the same purchase, seen from the paying side.

What each step means

StepWhen a part is on it
PlannedStill being priced. No Go, nothing asked for.
GoThe bid got a Go, but nothing is asked for yet.
RequestedA purchase request item buys it, with no P0 yet.
OrderedA P0 is on it, with no IPAS yet.
PayingIts P0 has an IPAS.
Cost finalThe IPAS's real cost for it is final. Only people who can see costs see this step.
ReceivedEvery item for it is received. "From stock" means the warehouse already had it.

A line with several parts shows its slowest part. A purchase item made from an S0 line only counts for a line that has exactly one part; with two or more parts, the app does not guess which one it buys.

If something looks wrong

  • All lines still have no badge: the staging site has not rebuilt yet. Wait a few minutes and reload.
  • Lines say Planned instead of Requested: the database change did not apply. Check "Apply migrations — staging" on GitHub.
  • No Attach P0 button: you are not signed in as the purchaser.
