=== REFERENCE - THE FOUR ACCESS LEVELS ===

full         Super Admin, Deal Team, Always sees
             Everything, minus Restricted Stages.

participant  Called in, window open
             Visits from the call-in forward. No money.

locked       Window closed
             Reference, project, current stage.

none         Everyone else
             Nothing. Not even a row.

Decided by sor_access_level() in Postgres. Not by the screen.
