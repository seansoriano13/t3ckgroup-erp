=== REFERENCE - FOUR ACCESS LEVELS ===

full         Super Admin, Deal Team, Always sees
             Everything except Restricted Stages.

participant  Called in, window open
             Visits from the call-in forward. No money.

locked       Window closed
             Reference, project, current stage.

none         Everyone else
             Nothing. Not even a row.

Decided by sor_access_level() in Postgres.


=== REFERENCE - WHAT OPENS AND CLOSES A WINDOW ===

Opened by  a task-creating Stage Action, an Assignment condition,
           or a Purchase Request.

Closed by  Only this Stage        leaving that Stage
           Until X begins         entering X
           Through X              leaving X
           The rest of the deal   a Terminal Stage

Also       one call-in = one window.
           Two call-ins on one person = two windows.
           A window only reaches FORWARD. Never shows what came before.
           A Mention grants nothing.
