### Decisions needed

**D01 Responsive tables below md** (UI-009, 165, 172, 181, 184, 185, 196, 200, 205, 210)
Tables keep desktop width (720–1880px) inside the card, so on phone/tablet only the first one or two columns show and status + the row action are off-screen.
- **A. Priority columns.** Hide low-value columns with `hidden md:table-cell` (ApprovalTable already does this for three columns) and keep status + action in view. *Consequence:* small diff per table, no new component; hidden data is reachable only by opening the row.
- **B. Stacked card rows below md.** *Consequence:* best phone experience; a new layout for each of ~9 tables, the most work and the most visual review.
- **C. Desktop-only, add a visible scroll cue.** *Consequence:* cheapest; phone users still scroll sideways, but they can tell there is more.
- **Recommend:** A for the queues employees open on phones (approvals, tasks, group-tasks, purchasing, bug-reports); C for admin tables (HR directory, activity log, templates, retail breakdown).

**D02 Mobile toolbars / cards overflow** (UI-170, 174, 175, 180, 183, 186, 190, 195, 197, 201, 207, 209, 223)
At 375 some controls are clipped or unreachable: HR working-week end time + day toggle, commission amounts, purchasing and tasks filters, the month pill over the page title.
- **A. Fix all of them for 375.** *Consequence:* ~10 layouts reworked and reviewed, several on pages nobody opens on a phone.
- **B. Fix employee-facing pages (tasks, my requests, purchasing, commission, target sizes); show a "best on desktop" notice on admin/config pages (settings HR, flow canvas, release-notes demos).** *Consequence:* about half the work; admin pages stay cramped on a phone but say so.
- **C. Declare the ERP desktop-first.** *Consequence:* no work; tasks and leave requests stay broken on the phones employees actually use.
- **Recommend:** B.

**D03 Tab-strip overflow & 768 sidebar** (UI-166, 167; closes dups 198, 202)
`TabGroup.jsx:52` hides its scrollbar, so clipped tabs give no hint they exist. Separately, the sidebar stays expanded at 768, leaving ~430px for content.
- **A. Edge fade + scroll the active tab into view.** *Consequence:* one file fixes every tab strip; tabs still scroll.
- **B. Wrap tabs onto two rows.** *Consequence:* everything visible; taller headers, and the underline variant looks broken across rows.
- **C. Overflow "More" menu.** *Consequence:* tidiest result; the most code and new behaviour to test.
- Sidebar: **collapse by default below 1024.** *Consequence:* tablets get the full width; opening nav costs one tap.
- **Recommend:** A, plus the sidebar collapse.

**D04 One-click destructive decisions** (UI-002, 137)
Leave rows: Decline and Approve fire immediately with an empty note, no confirm, no undo (bulk tasks do offer undo).
- **A. Undo toast (6s), as bulk tasks have.** *Consequence:* keeps the one-click speed; needs an undo path for head leave decisions in the service.
- **B. Approve stays one click; Decline opens the sheet, where the note is.** *Consequence:* no backend work; a mistaken approve still cannot be taken back by the head.
- **C. Remove row actions; decide in the sheet only (how the HR Leaves Manager works).** *Consequence:* safest and consistent with HR; slowest when clearing a queue.
- **Recommend:** B now; A later if the undo service is cheap.
- FAQ Editor "Clear all" (UI-137): replace the 3-second inline double-click with `confirmDeleteToast`. *Consequence:* same pattern as every other delete; one more click. **Recommend** doing it.

**D05 Action vocabulary** (UI-012, 013, 028, 072, 091, 106, 142, 143, 163)
One word per action. *Consequence of any choice:* copy-only diff; tests that assert these strings change with it.
- Task sent back: **Return** (bar, toast) vs Decline (bulk modal). Recommend **Return**; the modal is the outlier.
- Leave refused: Decline (head) vs Reject (HR; DB status is `REJECTED`). Recommend **Decline** in both UIs; DB untouched.
- Auth: Sign in / Sign out vs Log In / Logging out. Recommend **Sign in / Sign out**.
- Button casing: sentence ("Verify & sign") vs Title ("Save Changes"). Recommend **sentence case**; the newer screens use it.
- Stale boundary label: ">48h" vs "≥48h graded". Recommend whichever matches `isStaleSince`.
- Scope toggles ("My team / System-wide" vs "Company / My Tasks"): recommend **leave**; they describe different scopes.
- `/offsets` first tab "My Requests" under page title "My Requests": recommend **"Requests"**.
- Brand: "T3CKGROUP" (settings, `<title>`) vs "T3ckgroup" (login, reset). Your call; no recommendation.

**D06 Loading treatment** (UI-010, 035, 044, 071, 092, 109)
About eight variants: bare spinner at py-12/16/20, uppercase pulsing "LOADING X...", inline sentence, plain `<p>`, blank screen.
- **A. One shared `PageLoading` (spinner + sentence-case "Loading X…"), swapped in everywhere.** *Consequence:* consistent; a visual change on ~12 pages that needs a browser pass.
- **B. Skeleton rows per table.** *Consequence:* best perceived speed; a skeleton per table, the most work.
- **C. Leave.** *Consequence:* no risk; the inconsistency stays.
- **Recommend:** A.

**D07 Empty-state voice & component** (UI-011, 027, 042, 070, 079, 146, 189)
"Queue is Clear!" / "No Stale Backlog!" vs "Nothing waiting on you right now."; a local `EmptyState` copy in Leaves Manager; hand-built blocks in FAQ modal.
- **A. Sentence case, no "!", shared `EmptyState`, and a next-step action where one exists (e.g. "Open SOR Register").** *Consequence:* copy + component swaps in ~8 files; visual review.
- **B. Title Case with "!" (the older approvals style).** *Consequence:* fewer edits in approvals; the newer pages change instead.
- **C. Leave.**
- **Recommend:** A.

**D08 Pagination** (UI-032, 064, 065, 159, 182, 204)
Four pager implementations; SOR register (142 rows), HR directory (62), group-task grid (83) and bug reports render everything.
- **A. Lift ApprovalTable's pager into a shared `TablePager`; apply to SOR register and group-task grid.** *Consequence:* one pattern, grid matches its own table view; Ctrl+F no longer reaches rows on other pages.
- **B. Virtualise long tables.** *Consequence:* all rows stay "present"; new dependency, harder keyboard nav and sticky headers.
- **C. Leave until it is slow.** *Consequence:* no work; fine at today's volumes.
- **Recommend:** C for HR directory and bug reports, A for SORs + group-task grid. Leave the Leaves Manager 20/100/500 toggle (UI-065) as designed.

**D09 Unsaved-changes guard & URL state** (UI-112, 131, 138, 157, 221)
Profile and FAQ Editor hold edits in local state and drop them on navigation.
- **A. Shared `useUnsavedGuard(hasChanges)`: `beforeunload` + a router confirm.** *Consequence:* no silent loss; users see a prompt when leaving dirty.
- **B. Autosave.** *Consequence:* no prompt; needs debounced writes and failure handling (FAQ Editor writes the whole document).
- **C. Leave.**
- **Recommend:** A. Filters-in-URL for Retail and Activity Log (UI-131, 157): defer.

**D10 Retail "This month" KPI** (UI-199)
`monthOverMonth` (`utils/retailReport.js:333`) compares month-to-date with the whole previous month.
- **A. Compare with the same days of last month.** *Consequence:* an honest trend; label becomes "vs same period last month"; small util + test change.
- **B. Keep the math, label it "so far", drop the % until month end.** *Consequence:* trivial; loses the up/down signal most of the month.
- **C. Show last full month vs the one before.** *Consequence:* stable; no longer "this month".
- **Recommend:** A.

**D11 Sub-12px caption text** (UI-017, 031, 060, 084, 107, 119, 164, 222)
`text-[10px]` bold uppercase is the house caption style, built into `FieldBox` (`FieldBox.jsx:5`) and the `HEAD` / `LABEL` constants.
- **A. Keep the size; centralise it as one token.** *Consequence:* no visual change; a later resize is one line.
- **B. Raise captions to 11px.** *Consequence:* a small shift app-wide; some table headers may wrap.
- **C. 12px minimum.** *Consequence:* meets the guideline; dense tables and pills reflow, needing a full visual pass.
- **Recommend:** A. Exception: the `text-[9px]` badges in Activity Log → 10px.

**D12 Landing defaults** (UI-187, 211)
- `/hr/offsets` opens on Calendar with "Pending n" one tab away. **A. Open on Pending when the count is above 0.** *Consequence:* HR lands on the work; changes the landing chosen in the redesign. **B. Keep.** Recommend A.
- Sidebar "My Tasks" opens a page titled "All Tasks" on Company scope for HR. **A. Default HR to the My Tasks scope.** *Consequence:* label and page agree; HR needs one click for the company view. **B. Rename the nav item "Tasks".** *Consequence:* no behaviour change; non-HR lose the "My". Recommend A.

**D13 Auth pages: validation & theming** (UI-088, 090)
- Validation is toast-only. **A. Inline errors + `aria-invalid` + focus the first bad field.** *Consequence:* proper form behaviour; more markup on two small forms. **B. Keep the toast, add `required` and focus.** *Consequence:* tiny; browser-native bubbles appear. **C. Leave.** Recommend A.
- `bg-white` panels: check `/login` in dark theme first. If the text is unreadable, swap to `bg-card` (then it is a fix). If login is meant to stay light, force light there instead.
